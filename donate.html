Your Support Saves Health

Small contributions can protect dignity and lives

💔 Why Donations Matter

Many women cannot afford safe menstrual products due to high prices and taxes. As a result, they are forced to use unhygienic alternatives, increasing the risk of infections and serious health issues.

Donations help provide access to safe menstrual products, educational resources, and awareness programs in communities where women are most vulnerable.

Where Your Donation Goes

🩸 Safe Menstrual Products
📚 Health Education
🤝 Community Support
